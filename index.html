SafetyFirst Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Developer: emberrose, contact: mendozarnika@gmail.com. This policy applies to the SafetyFirst Android app.

What the app does

SafetyFirst lets you protect apps on your phone with your fingerprint (or device PIN/pattern).

Information stored on your device only

  • List of apps you choose to lock – stored only on your device.
  • Which app is in the foreground – the app uses Android "Usage access" to detect when a locked app opens. This is processed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Fingerprint data – SafetyFirst never sees, stores or receives your fingerprint. Verification is done by Android; the app only receives a yes/no result.

Permissions

  • Usage access – to know when a locked app is opened.
  • Display over other apps – to show the lock screen above a locked app.
  • Foreground service / notification – to keep protection running.
  • Biometric – to ask Android to verify your fingerprint.
  • Run at startup – to restart protection after reboot.
  • Internet – to show ads and process your subscription.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to show ads and measure performance, and may use cookies/similar technologies. Where required (e.g., EEA, UK) we ask for your consent first. See Google's policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's Google settings. Premium removes ads.

Subscription and payments

Premium (PHP 399 per month, auto-renewing) is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. Manage or cancel any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob and Google Play) as described above.

Children

SafetyFirst is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Security

Sensitive keys are kept in Android's hardware-backed Keystore. App data is excluded from cloud backup.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored on your device. Contact us at mendozarnika@gmail.com with questions.

Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes.
